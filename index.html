Happy Birthday Dad!! ❤️❤️🥳

Just wanted to wish you a very very happy birthday and thank you for everything you've done for us over the years!! I know I don't say this enough but I really really love and appreciate you so much and I'm so grateful for everything you've done for our family.

I think as I've grown older I've come to appreciate more and more just how much you've sacrificed to give us the life we have today. I'll always remember the days when we were living in Hougang and you would bring me to school on the crowded train while carrying my heavy school bag for me and making sure I was comfortable. And thinking about how far we've come since then to having such a large and comfortable home in Telok Kurau really makes me appreciate just how hard you've worked over the years to provide for all of us. I know none of this came easy and that there must have been so many sacrifices you've made along the way that we probably don't even know about.

And it's not just the big things but all the little things you do for us every single day that mean so much to me. From driving us to school to always making sure we have everything we need to even remembering all our favourite fruits and making sure they're always stocked up at home (sometimes a bit too much HAHAHA). I think these are things that are so easy to take for granted when growing up but looking back I realise just how lucky we are to have a dad who cares so much about us and always puts our happiness and comfort before his own.

I also really admire how you've always worked so hard for the family and no matter how busy or tired you might be you've always made sure that we're well taken care of. Even if you are tired you always hep us fix things in our room, with the house and so on. Without your help, it would be hard for us to always stay comfortable and focused on our studies. You've given us so many opportunities and experiences growing up and I know that so much of what I've been able to do today is because of all the hard work and sacrifices you and Mum have made for us. I really hope that one day I'll be able to give back to you even a fraction of what you've given me.

More than anything I just want you to know that none of what you do goes unnoticed and that I really appreciate and love you so so much even if I don't always express it. Thank you for being such an amazing dad and for always being there for us. I really couldn't have asked for a better father and I'm so grateful for the childhood and life you've given us ❤️

Wishing you a very very Happy Birthday Dad!! I hope this year brings you lots of happiness and good health and many more wonderful memories with all of us. I also hope that as we grow older you'll get to worry a little less about us and spend more time enjoying yourself because you really deserve it after everything you've done for the family.

I really look up to you Dad and I hope that when I have a family of my own one day I'll be able to be as good of a father to my children as you've been to us. You've really set such a wonderful example for me to follow and I hope I can make you proud.

Love you so so much Dad and thank you again for absolutely everything!!

With all my love,Vignesh ♡
